👁️ VisioMate AI

Voice assistant for the visually impaired. Press the big button (or the M key) and speak.

Conversation

Quick actions

Camera starts the first time you use detection or text reading. Text language:

🧭 Navigation

Or say: "navigate to <place>", then "next step".

💊 Medicine reminders

    Or say: "remind me to take tablet at 8:30 pm". Keep this page open for alerts to sound.

    🆘 Emergency SOS

    You get 5 seconds to cancel. Your phone's message app opens with your location ready to send. A web page cannot send SMS silently.

    ⚠️ Assistive aid only, not a replacement for a white cane or a guide. Voice needs Chrome or Edge. Source: GitHub
